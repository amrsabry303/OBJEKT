# objekt product naming

Decided with Sabry, 29 to 30 Oct 2026. Names are for **products**, not brands. Brand names stay as in CLAUDE.md.

## The territory (decided)

Designer-led names that feel engraved under a collectible object. Not literal descriptions ("Metal Tray"), not poetry ("Silver Lining"), not generic Scandinavian minimalism.

1. **Short and easy to say in English and Arabic.** Aim for two syllables, 4 to 6 letters.
2. **Rooted in the object's silhouette, geometry or construction.** Decide from the product photo, not from its description.
3. **One root per name.** No near-twins (LUNA and LUNO, PLIA and PLETO, PIRA and PICO).
4. **Mix languages.** Not every name French or Italian; include plain English words (PEBBLE, POD, TOTEM, PLEAT) and coinages, or the collection looks derivative.
5. **No childish sounds** (BUBA, PODO, PEBLO, BOTO). Test every name against the real design.
6. **Not every product needs an invented name.** A consistent philosophy beats forcing one on all 50. Some products can keep a plain name ("Mirror Tray").
7. **Format on the site:** `NAME Product type`, for example `ONDA Coffee Table`, with the Arabic name and type beside it where the page allows.
8. **Spelling option:** Sabry allows "c" to be written "k" (objekt, SOKLE, KONO). Use it only where the word still reads well; FAKETTE read as "fake" and was dropped.
9. **Material or finish in the title only when it defines the product,** never a selectable variant.

## Cautions

- **None of these are trademark-cleared.** Short common words (LUNA, ONDA, BLOK, TORSO, ARRIS, PODIUM, OCULUS) are in wide use as brands. Say so every time names are shown, and send the final list to Sabry's lawyer.
- Locked decision 7 (English word or abstract coinage only) was written for brand names. Sabry has not said whether it covers product names; many candidates are Spanish or Italian words. Ask once if it matters.
- Never invent product details to justify a name. Sizes and materials in the Shopify template are estimates marked "to confirm".

## Where names go

- Title, handle and SEO title in `_plan/shopify-import-template.csv` (rows are per product; variant rows keep the product handle).
- The product record in the objekt workspace (notes), once Sabry approves a name.
- Slides in the OBJEKT reference book (title line).

## Steps

1. Look at the product photo and record in one line what the silhouette is.
2. Pick from the candidate table; if none fits, propose two names that meet the territory rules.
3. Check for near-twins in the list and for childish sounds.
4. Show Sabry the shortlist and wait for a choice. Do not rename products in Shopify or the workspace on your own.
5. After approval, update the template and tell him names are uncleared.
