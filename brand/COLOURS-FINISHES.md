## Finishes
- Painted surfaces: paint system (Electrostatic powder coating | Duco paint) + paint finish (Matte | Gloss, subject to the system).
- Exposed metal: Brushed | Mirror.
- Spec format: **paint system + colour + finish**, e.g. "Electrostatic powder coating · Carbon · Matte", "Duco · Oxblood · Gloss". This replaces the blanket "Matte/Gloss lacquer" wording.
- Ink and Carbon are two different colours (navy vs soft black). Do not merge them.

Bone: White 98, Yellow oxide 1.3, Raw umber 0.7 · Putty: White 83, Raw umber 11, Yellow oxide 4, Black 2 · Carbon: Black 95, White 5 · Ink: Ultramarine 62, Black 20, White 18 · Oxblood: Crimson red 62, Burnt umber 28, Black 10 · Clay: Red oxide 55, Yellow oxide 15, White 30 · Chocolate: Burnt umber 88, Red oxide 10, Black 2 · Objekt Green: Phthalo green 22, Yellow oxide 30, White 42, Raw umber 6.
