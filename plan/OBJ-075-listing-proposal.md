## Name (none is approved or trademark-cleared)

Silhouette: a plain round cylinder with a low foot ring, three sizes. Format `NAME Product type`.

1. **DRUM Floor Planter** (recommended): plain English, short, says the shape; Arabic ... درام. Already in the Product Sheet. Common word, so clearance is open.
2. **CASK Floor Planter**: English, short; a little heavier than the shape.
3. **SILO Floor Planter**: short, easy in Arabic; reads taller than S and M, which are as wide as high.

## Description (draft; Arabic for a native reader to check)

Short: A plain round planter in brushed stainless steel. Three sizes, one shape.

Long (EN): DRUM is a plain steel cylinder. It has no ornament and no mark. A low foot ring lifts it off the floor. It is rolled from 2 mm 304 stainless steel, welded once and brushed. Drain holes in the base let water out. It takes a standard nursery pot: about 15 L in S, 30 L in M and 50 L in L. Plant and pot are not included. Made to order, 30 days.
