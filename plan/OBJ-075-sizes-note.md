# OBJ-075 floor planter, three sizes: industry check and proposal (12 Nov 2026)

**Proposal, not costed, Production to confirm.** Sabry's brief (12 Nov): "this pot in 3 sizes. Check the industry sizes." Reference: `images/OBJ-075_ref-sabry-12nov.jpg` (two polished stainless cylinders, maker unknown, reference only). One product, three size variants.

## 1. What the public sources say (fetched 12 Nov 2026)

**Nursery grower pots (the liners the planters must take).** Sizes are not standardised: pages say "sizes may vary between pot manufacturers", and Speciality Trees says a "40cm pot" is sold as 40 or 45 L but holds about 27 L. Top diameter x height, as listed:

| 7 | 250 x 200 mm | Grasslands, Johnsons |
| 10 | 280 x 225 mm | Grasslands, Jacksons (Johnsons shows 115 for the height, which looks like a typo) |
| 15 | 330 x 300 mm | Grasslands, Johnsons |
| 20 | 355 x 325 mm | Grasslands, Johnsons |
| 25 | 385 x 350 mm | Grasslands, Johnsons |
| 30 | 410 x 360 mm | Grasslands, Johnsons |
| 40 | 500 x 450 mm | Grasslands, Johnsons |
| 50 | 500 x 390 mm (yougarden), 490 x 380 mm (a UK grower); Grasslands lists 550 x 500 mm | search results, not fetched pages |
| 100 | sources disagree: 610 x 480 mm (Paramount) vs 520 x 560 mm (Winterhill) | search results only |

**What size of plant goes in which pot** (Nursery Near Me, Australia, tree height at dispatch): 200 to 250 mm pot, 50 cm to 1 m; 300 to 330 mm pot (about 13 to 18 L), 1 to 1.6 m; 400 to 500 mm pot (about 35 to 90 L), 1.5 to 2.5 m; 75 to 110 L bag (45 to 55 cm across), 2 to 3.5 m. Crocus: 10 to 15 L = semi-mature shrubs and trees of 3 to 5 years; 20 to 100+ L = fully established plants. Tree height depends on species and age, so these are guides, not rules.

**Other makers' floor planter ranges.**
- London Stone cylinder (corten, not stainless): 300 x 300, 400 x 400, 500 x 500 mm, 15 mm hidden feet. Their page did not load for me; figures come from the search result.
- Taylor Made Planters (powder-coated steel cylinder): diameters 400, 600, 800 mm, heights 400 to 1000 mm (search result).
- Metalco Aster (steel, 304 option): D550 x 580, D750 x 730, D950 x 980, D1200 x 1430 mm (search result; too large for us).
- Kent Stainless Corporate Office Cylinder (fetched): grade 304, typically D800 x 750, satin 320 grit, custom sizes, stainless risers for a pallet truck, the planting contractor adds a geotextile insert; wall thickness not stated.
- Lump (AU, fetched): custom cylinders, drainage holes optional; up to D1500 x 600.
- Read: commercial cylinders run 300 to 800 mm across, mostly about 1 : 1 height to diameter. The photo's pots are taller (about 1.25 to 1.3 by eye), so ours are slimmer than the market norm.

**Material.** 304 density 7,900 kg/m3 (Austral Wright, mechanical-engineering.com); another page gives 7.93 g/cm3. Weights below use 7.9. Standard sheet sizes 1000 x 2000, 1250 x 2500, 1500 x 3000 mm (Righton Blackburns, Acerinox, Savoy Piping). We assume 1250 x 2500 x 2.

## 2. Recommended ladder (proposal)

Planter inner diameter = outer minus 4 mm (2 mm wall). Overall height includes a 12 mm foot ring. Ratios kept near the photo.

| Size | D x H (mm) | H/D | Liner it takes | Clearance | Steel weight, about | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| S | 300 x 360 | 1.20 | 10 L (D280 x 225), also 7 L | 16 mm | 6.4 kg | Smallest pot the photo shows; plants up to about 1 m (a 200 to 250 mm nursery pot class). Sits on a console, in a corner, or beside L. |
| M | 400 x 500 | 1.25 | 20 L (D355) or 25 L (D385) | 11 mm over 25 L | 11.8 kg | Mid-height plants of 1 to 1.6 m; the 15 to 18 L class would fit loosely inside. The 30 L (D410) does not fit. |
| L | 520 x 650 | 1.25 | 40 L (D500 x 450), 50 L if D500 | 16 mm | 20.0 kg | The photo's tall plant: nursery 400 to 500 mm pots hold 1.5 to 2.5 m trees. D500 is the common top diameter of 40 and 50 L pots; D520 clears it. Not for the 100 L class. |

Steps are about x1.3 in diameter. Sheet check, 1250 x 2500 x 2: size L wall blank 1627 x 638, base D516 and ring strip 1565 x 12 all fit one sheet (sheet 4 of the drawing). Size M wall blank is 1250.4 long, so it runs along the 2500 length. Weight of L about 20 kg steel only, plus liner and soil; weight in use is for Production and the shipping line, not claimed here.

## 3. Construction frame (proposal)

Rolled 2 mm 304, one longitudinal weld ground flush, base disc welded inside the wall bottom, low foot ring (12 mm high, set in 10 mm) welded under the base, no drain hole, loose liner (earlier decision for OBJ-173 and OBJ-075). The photo shows the foot ring on the smaller pot only; the proposal puts it on all three for one family.

**Open point for Production.** The liner is shorter than the planter (S 98 mm, M 113 mm, L 163 mm short of rim height, arithmetic), so it needs a riser to bring the rim to about 25 mm under the planter rim. The riser is not designed.

## 4. Finish: recommend launching brushed

Brushed stainless first. Reasons: mirror pieces are capped by polisher capacity (CONTEXT, day-1 list rule 5; nobody has counted the polishers), and a ground longitudinal weld on a rolled cylinder is hard to hide on mirror. Mirror as a second colourway once Production confirms slots; the photo is mirror, so renders can show both. Sabry decides.
