## Rules behind them
These are standard retail range-planning rules, not drawn from your sales (there are none yet). Review after 8 weeks of orders.
1. No family above 30% of the list, no single category above 10%.
2. A launched category has at least 2 products. Singles only where the library holds one.
3. Price ladder inside each family: about 40% entry, 40% core, 20% statement. Relative; nothing here is costed.
4. Replaced 29 Oct by the production-process mix below (agreed with Youssef). Upholstery and electrics still wait for wave 2.
5. Mirror-finish products capped after the polishers are counted.
6. Large or heavy pieces (over 1 m or about 15 kg) about a fifth of the list, because delivery covers all of Egypt.
7. Each room (living, dining, bedside and desk, entry, balcony) gets at least 6 products.
8. At least 10 small pieces so a first customer can buy under the large ones.
9. One design language per family. No low-poly faceted animals.
10. Rebalance monthly from what sells.

## Production-process mix (29 Oct 2026, from Sabry's meeting with Youssef)

Purpose: protect production capacity. Sized on a 60-product day 1; scale down in proportion if the list is nearer 40.

| Process | Products | Share | What it means |
|---|---|---|---|
| Metal | about 30 | about 50% | Laser cut, fold, weld. The easiest for the factory. |
| of which curved or highly technical | at most 10 | | Curves take time. The other 20 or more are straight cut and fold. |
| 3D print, then mould | about 18 | about 30% | Print the pattern (or print the mould itself), then cast or lay up in the mould. |
| 3D printed directly | 5 to 10 | 8 to 17% | The print is the product. |

The three rows add up to 53 to 58, inside the 40 to 60 target. This replaces the old "about 60% easy to make" rule.

Open:
- The category targets above and this process mix are two separate cuts of the same list. Every day-1 product needs a process tag before they can be checked together.
- The group decision says 3D printing is a process, not a brand, for patterns, scale models and one-off catalogue pieces. Printed-direct products fit that only as a small catalogue; keep it at 5 to 10.
- Moulded products need the print and the mould built first, so they are the long lead items. Mirror polish (metal) still queues behind the polishers.
- Turned-form candleholders, vases and pods on the list (for example OBJ-004, 141, 147, 154) look like moulded or printed pieces, not metal, so the metal count may be lower than the category list suggests.
