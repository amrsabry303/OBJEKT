# Prompt: set up the objekt website categories

You are helping Sabry set up the product categories for the objekt online store (Shopify, Egypt first, launch Friday 20 November 2026). Another Claude session manages the product workspace; this prompt is the structure it settled on, so please build the site to match it.

## Context
- objekt is the single launch brand, testing the waters. That is why it sells furniture and a large sculpture as well as small objects.
- Day 1 is about 46 products now (target 40 to 60), tracked in the "objekt workspace" artifact (https://claude.ai/artifact/HysZRbjoMyneXH8Xbiipcn).
- Customers are in Egypt. Every page needs English and Arabic. Prices will include 14% VAT and are not set yet. Most products are made to order, so lead time is shown, not stock.

## The structure: 4 website categories (customer menu)
1. **Furniture**: coffee tables, side tables and bedside tables, stools and benches, pedestals and plinths, chairs and lounge seating.
2. **Table & Living** (name may change to "Tabletop"): trays, bowls and baskets, coasters and trivets, tissue and paper holders, desk and book.
3. **Objects**: candleholders, ashtrays and incense holders, vases, decorative and statement sculpture, shelves, wall art and photo displays, mirrors.
4. **Planters**: floor planters, tabletop planters, planter screens.

Lighting is wave 2 and joins Objects later. A fifth category "Wall" (shelves, wall art, mirrors) may be split off from Objects when lighting launches or the list passes 60 products.

## What to set up
- Four Shopify collections with the names above, in this order, with an English and Arabic title and a one-line description each.
- Use the sub-category (for example "Coffee Tables", "Vases") as the Shopify **Product type**, so it can drive filters inside each collection.
- Add tags for: process (metal, curved metal, moulded, printed), finish (mirror polish, brushed, lacquered, powder-coated), colour (names from the objekt colour guide: Bone, Putty, Carbon, Ink, Oxblood, Clay, Objekt Green), and room (living, dining, bedside, desk, entry, balcony). Room, material, finish, colour and price are **filters, not categories**, so the menu stays at four.
- Menu: the four categories only. No category with fewer than about 8 products should appear as its own menu item.
- Do not publish products, prices or collections live. Prepare them as drafts for Sabry to review.

## Rules
- Do not invent prices, costs, sizes or lead times. None are set yet; leave them blank.
- Product photos are reference pictures from the workspace, not product images. Do not use them on the live site. Leave images blank for now.
- Several products are one product with variants: OBJ-166 (two heights), OBJ-176 (four colour versions), OBJ-175 (modular panels). Set them up as one product with variants.
- Product names marked "(name to come)" have only a reference photo. Leave the title blank for Sabry.
