# POLYCRAFT — project context

Separate from Dough client work. Nothing here touches the Dough systems.

## What this is
Sabry is partnering with **Youssef Gaber**, owner of Polycraft — a Cairo fabrication
factory (sheet metal: laser/fold/weld/mirror-polish; GRP composite moulding; automotive
paint; large-format 3D printing; upholstery) — to build a holding company that owns
three design brands. The factory stays Youssef's and supplies the group.

## Locked decisions — do not relitigate
1. Market: **Egypt first.** Export is phase three.
2. **Factory stays Youssef's**, outside the HoldCo, under a Master Manufacturing Agreement.
   He is paid twice: factory margin + HoldCo equity.
3. Capital is **being raised** — ask is $217k–570k over 18 months.
4. **Unlicensed character work (KAWS, LV monogram) is sunset entirely.**
5. **Three brands**, by buyer — 01 Sculpture · 02 Furniture · 03 Objects. Not more.
6. Launch order **03 → 02 → 01**. Design in parallel, launch in sequence.
7. Names: **English word or abstract coinage only.** No Arabic/bilingual branding now.
   Creative director creates names; Sabry chooses; lawyer clears.
8. Earlier name proposals (Zawaya / Vertex / Athar) were **rejected**. Use Brand 01/02/03.
9. Material (fibre vs steel) is a **spec, not a brand**. Same design, two prices, one name.
10. 3D printing is a **process, not a brand** — patterns, scale models, one-off catalogue only.
11. **Only Brand 01 gets a symbol.** 02 and 03 are wordmark-only.

## Brand 03 — current definition (newer than the documents)
Statement design objects, NOT gift-shop offcut pieces. Eight categories: tabletop, vessels,
candle & incense, desk & book, stools & pedestals, wall objects, lighting, small sculpture.
EGP 4k–90k. Offcuts are a sourcing efficiency, not the headline. Boundaries:
- vs Brand 01: Objects' small sculpture is tabletop ≤500 mm, open range, **not numbered**.
  Numbered editions belong to Brand 01 only.
- vs Brand 02: Objects is bought one at a time and placed; Furniture is bought in
  quantity for a scheme, or installed. A placed lamp is Objects; a hardwired pendant is 02.
The plan and briefs in `_plan/` still carry the OLD Brand 03 framing — update pending.

## objekt is the launch brand (30 Oct 2026, Sabry)
objekt is the single launch brand and is testing the waters, which is why it carries furniture
and large sculpture as well as objects on day 1. The brand split above (Furniture / Objects /
Sculpture) is the later structure, not the day-1 one. Day 1 is 40-60 products across 6 internal
families and 4 website categories (Furniture, Table & Living, Objects, Planters); see
`_plan/objekt-assortment-targets.md`.

## Hard constraints discovered
- The group **cannot be named Polycraft** (brand equity would accrue to an asset it
  doesn't own). Polycraft becomes the industrial identity; "Made at Polycraft, Cairo"
  is the shared provenance line.
- **Polisher capacity is the bottleneck** — the 2–3 people who can hit an A-grade
  mirror finish. All three brands queue behind them.
- **FX mismatch**: dollar inputs, pound revenue. Every quote carries a material index
  clause and 30-day validity.
- Low-poly faceted animals are a **commodity** and cannot lead the group.
- **No price in any document is costed.** They are ladders. Never quote from them.
