## 6. How Sabry likes to work (from the memory notes)
- Short one-line decisions; he is often on his phone.
- Draw the reference **exactly** unless he says free or custom.
- "Approved" on a drawing triggers a fixed routine: change the stamp to "Approved by Sabry <date>. Production review pending. Not engineered", regenerate, zip, upload PDF + pack to the product's Drive folder, update the workspace record and the slide sticker, add to the green list. Check the PDF text has no "DRAFT" before any upload.
- Verify before claiming: render PDFs to PNG and look, read DXFs back, view every AI render before sending it. Report failures plainly.
- Magnific: run one generation at a time; the model takes backdrop words literally; check proportions of every render against the drawing.
- Never post, send or upload on his behalf without a clear yes.
