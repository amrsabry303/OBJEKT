Sabry supplied the official list on 1 Nov 2026: Bone #E9E4D8, Putty #B9B2A5, Carbon #222222, Ink #1E2A44 (navy), Oxblood #5A1F25, Clay #A8553A, Chocolate #4A2A1B, Objekt Green #37613F (signature). Ink and Carbon are distinct.
Finishes: paint system (electrostatic powder coating or Duco) + colour + finish (Matte or Gloss); exposed metal Brushed or Mirror. Spec format "system · colour · finish"; replaces "Matte/Gloss lacquer".

**Why:** product colours in the workspace, production book and Shopify must use one vocabulary.
**How to apply:** only these names; hex are screen approximations until physical samples. Details in POLYCRAFT/_plan/brand/COLOURS-FINISHES.md. Related: [[objekt-brand-guidelines]].
