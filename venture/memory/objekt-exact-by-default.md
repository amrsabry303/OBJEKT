Always do the exact design of the reference, unless Sabry specifies otherwise for that product. New products get `freedom: exact` and `ours: true` in the workspace decisions.

**Why:** he said "always do the exact design unless i specified otherwise" right after OBJ-164 (a Temu corner shelf) was queued and I had set it to free. This replaces my earlier rule that another maker's piece is never drawn exact (see [[objekt-drawings-standard]]).

**How to apply:** default exact; only use free or custom when he says so. Keep the reference source in the notes. The 22 x 22 cm style sizes from a listing are still "estimated from the photo".
