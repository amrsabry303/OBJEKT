Day 1 (about 60 products): about 30 metal (about 50%, the easiest), of which at most 10 curved or highly technical; about 18 made by 3D printing then moulding (print the pattern or the mould); 5 to 10 directly 3D printed.

**Why:** Sabry met Youssef on 29 Oct 2026 and changed the mix to protect production capacity. It replaces the old "about 60% easy to make" rule in `_plan/objekt-assortment-targets.md`.

**How to apply:** tag every day-1 product with a process (metal straight, metal curved, print then mould, print direct) and check counts against these numbers alongside the category targets. Flag any list that breaks them. Related: [[polycraft-venture-constraints]].
