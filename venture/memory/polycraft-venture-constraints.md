Sabry is partnering with **Youssef Gaber**, owner of the Polycraft fabrication factory (Cairo — sheet metal, GRP composite, large-format 3D printing, automotive finishing), to build a holding company owning three design brands. Decided 10 Sept 2026 — treat as settled:

1. **Market: Egypt first.** Not GCC-led, not export-first.
2. **Factory stays Youssef's** — outside the HoldCo, supplying under a Master Manufacturing Agreement. He is paid twice: factory margin *and* HoldCo equity.
3. **Capital being raised**, $217k–570k over 18 months.
4. **Unlicensed character work (KAWS, LV monogram) sunset entirely.**
5. **Three brands, by buyer**: 01 Sculpture (catalogue → signature → commissioned), 02 Furniture (metal indoor + GRP outdoor), 03 Objects (metal small goods from offcuts).
6. **Launch order: 03 → 02 → 01.** Design all three in parallel, launch in sequence.
7. **Brand names: English word or abstract coinage only.** No Arabic, no bilingual lockups. The creative director creates the names; Sabry chooses; his lawyer clears.
8. **Final naming, 17 Sept 2026 (superseded an intermediate SUBJEKT/objekts round from earlier the same day): Furniture = subjekt, Objects = objekt, Sculpture = JEKT** — all three brands now named, lowercase for the two sub-brands, uppercase for JEKT. One shared "-JEKT" root across all three, three different casings/prefixes. Sabry was told, and is aware, that "objekt" (no trailing s) is the exact spelling that collides with an established international design magazine running since 1991 (OBJEKT International) — he chose to proceed anyway. Sculpture (Brand 01) is the only brand with a symbol, not just a wordmark, per the original naming brief — that symbol still needs designing for "JEKT." Earlier proposals (Zawaya/Vertex/Athar) were rejected.
9. **Material (fibre vs steel) is a spec, never a second brand.** Same design, two prices, one name.
10. **3D printing is a process, not a brand** — patterns, scale models, and one-off catalogue pieces only.

**Why:** Each was chosen over stated alternatives. Re-proposing GCC-first, a factory contribution, more brands, bilingual branding or new name suggestions is relitigating a settled decision.

**Team (as of 17 Sept 2026):** Sabry ("the brains") and Youssef ("the muscles," the factory) are the two founders. **Nour** is probably coming on as **lead product designer** — she coordinates directly with Sabry on design direction, inspiration references and files; she is the intended recipient of the per-product design briefs (dimensions, materials, visual character), not the full research/strategy document. Youssef gets a short, plain-language operations brief instead of the full document — see [[polycraft-deliverables]] for the Youssef-facing one-pager.

**How to apply:** Decision 2's hard consequence — the group **cannot be named Polycraft**; Polycraft is rebranded as the *industrial* identity and "made at Polycraft, Cairo" becomes the shared provenance line. Only Brand 01 gets a symbol; 02 and 03 are wordmark-only. See [[polycraft-folder-mixed-provenance]] and [[polycraft-deliverables]].
