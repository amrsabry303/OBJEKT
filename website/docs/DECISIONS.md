| D-025 | **Sold-out ready variants are switched by hand (founder, 30 Oct 2026, QA-04).** Shopify's Fulfilment filter matches a variant's `custom.fulfilment` even when the variant is sold out, so a ready piece that sells out would still appear under "Ready to deliver". Staff handle it in the product admin: when a ready variant sells out, open that variant, set Fulfilment to `made_to_order`, fill in the lead time weeks (min and max), and turn on continue selling when out of stock (a ready variant has it off, so it would stay unbuyable); when stock returns, set it back to `ready` and turn continue selling off. Not automated; Shopify Flow stays an option later. | cheapest and clearest at launch volume (most products are made to order, D-023); keeps card, filter and button in agreement | **Approved (founder)**; risk: a forgotten switch shows a sold-out variant under the Ready filter |
