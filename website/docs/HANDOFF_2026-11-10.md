## 5. Shipping: proposed setup (needs Sabry's numbers before any write)
Sabry asked for help designing per-size shipping. Proposal to confirm with him:

1. **Size classes.** Four classes, each a Shopify **shipping profile**: S (fits a small parcel: tabletop objekts, candleholders), M (boxed: planters, lamps-size pieces), L (one-person carry furniture: side tables, stools), XL (freight: tables, shelving, sofas). Each product joins exactly one profile. Sabry or Polycraft assigns the class per OBJ code; packed size per product is the real input.
2. **Zones.** Shopify shipping zones per profile: Egypt; then country groups Sabry chooses (for example Gulf, Europe, UK, USA and Canada, Rest of world). Each zone × class gets a flat rate in EGP from Sabry's carrier quotes.
3. **Free above 70,000.** Use one **automatic free-shipping discount** with a minimum purchase of 70,000 EGP, all countries. Do not use per-profile "free over" rate conditions: Shopify applies those to each profile's subtotal, not the order total, so a mixed cart would charge shipping wrongly.
4. **Mixed carts.** Shopify adds the rates of each profile in the cart. Decide with Sabry whether two items of the same class should charge once or per item (Shopify charges once per profile per order by default with flat rates).
5. **Alternative** if the carrier prices by weight: give every product a shipping weight (or volumetric weight) and use weight-band rates per zone instead of classes. Ask Sabry which way his carrier quotes.
6. **XL abroad.** Freight furniture abroad may be better as "shipping quoted after order". Ask Sabry; if yes, exclude XL from international zones and add a contact-for-quote line on those products.

Inputs to get from Sabry before writing anything: carrier(s); the zone list; a rate table (zone × class, EGP); the class for each launch product; currency and wording of the 70,000 threshold; whether customs are paid by the customer (rec. yes, stated on the site).

Then, with his yes: create profiles and zones (Admin API `deliveryProfileCreate` / `deliveryProfileUpdate`, check names with `graphql_schema` first), the free-shipping discount (`discountAutomaticFreeShippingCreate`), and test checkout rates for Egypt plus two foreign countries with one S, one XL and one mixed cart, over and under 70,000.
