# Project brief

**Brand:** OBJEKT — contemporary furniture and home objects, Egypt. Sculptural presence, material honesty, thoughtful proportion, expressive character. An everyday object (vase, table, console, tray) should be useful and also a focal point.

**Personality:** architectural, contemporary, artistic, sophisticated, warm, confident, understated but never boring, playful through form and composition rather than decoration. Not an old-fashioned luxury retailer; no gold, no ornament, no formal language.

**Experience goal:** editorial first, commerce second, and commerce never frictionless-compromised. A design publication + gallery + concept store that happens to run on Shopify.

**Business objectives**
1. Sell both stock (Ready to Deliver) and manufactured-after-purchase (Made to Order) pieces from one catalogue, sometimes within a single product (per variant).
2. Make the difference unmistakable and the delivery promise accurate.
3. Let non-technical staff add products, collections, stories and rearrange the homepage with no code.
4. Local payment and delivery reality (EGP, Egyptian gateways) treated as a verification item, not an assumption.

**References (conceptual weighting, not rules):** Zara Home 35% shopping UX · FRAMA 30% art direction · 101 Copenhagen 25% product presentation · Audo 10% configuration/information. New Works for architectural restraint. Browsing of these sites was **not** performed in this session; the direction comes from the brief's descriptions. Nothing is copied.

**Out of scope until founder says so:** invented brand history, designer names, sustainability or production claims, real pricing, lead times, payment gateway choice, a logo.

**Open inputs needed from founder:** official logo files; photography (or a shoot plan); real product list, materials, finishes, lead times; Shopify store/plan; payment gateway; deposit policy for made-to-order; domain.
