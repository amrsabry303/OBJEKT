## Refund policy

Made-to-order deposit
Made-to-order pieces take a 50% deposit at order, by card through checkout or by InstaPay. The remaining 50% is paid on delivery, in cash or by InstaPay. The deposit is non-refundable.

Returns
- Ready pieces. Returnable within 48 hours of delivery, unused. Return delivery is paid by you. Refunds are made by the same method you paid with, within 7 days of receiving the piece back.
- Made-to-order pieces. Not returnable, except for damage or a defect.

Damage
If a piece arrives damaged, tell us within 48 hours of delivery on WhatsApp (+20 100 082 2240), with photos. You choose what happens next: repair, replacement or refund.
